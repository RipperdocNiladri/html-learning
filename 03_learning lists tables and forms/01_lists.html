Steps to Make the Perfect Cupcake

Ingredients & Tools

  • Dry:
    1/2 cups all-purpose flour, 1 tsp baking powder, 1/4 tsp salt.
  • Wet:
    1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter (softened), 1 cup granulated sugar, 2 large eggs, 1.5 tsp vanilla extract, 3/4 cup milk.
  • Equipment:
    12-cup standard muffin pan, paper cupcake liners, electric hand mixer or whisk, rubber spatula, mixing bowls

Step-by-Step Instructions

  1. Prep the Oven and Pan: Preheat your oven to 350°F (175°C). Line your muffin tin with paper cupcake liners.
  2. Combine Dry Ingredients: In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and salt until there are no clumps. Set aside.
  3. Cream Butter and Sugar: In a large bowl, use an electric mixer to beat the softened butter and sugar together on medium speed for 2 to 3 minutes, until the mixture is light, fluffy, and pale in color.
  4. Add Wet Ingredients: Add the eggs one at a time, beating well after each addition. Mix in the vanilla extract
  5. Mix Wet and Dry: Turn the mixer to low speed. Add about half of your dry flour mixture, followed by about half of the milk. Mix until just combined, then add the remaining flour and milk. Scrape the sides and bottom of the bowl with a rubber spatula so everything is incorporated.
  6. Fill the Liners: Scoop the batter into the cupcake liners, filling each one about 2/3 to 3/4 full to prevent overflowing.
  7. Bake: Bake for 15 to 20 minutes. To check for doneness, insert a toothpick into the center of a cupcake; if it comes out clean or with a few moist crumbs, they are done
  8. Cool: Allow the cupcakes to cool in the pan for 5 minutes. Then, transfer them to a wire rack to cool completely before frosting.
